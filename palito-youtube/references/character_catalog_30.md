# 40-Character Bean-Body Catalog (`#1` to `#40`)

All 40 characters share the core **Bean-Body Vector Rig** from the 4 reference sheets (`Gemini_Generated_Image_*.jpeg`) while obeying the **Mouthless Rule** of the original video style (no mouth line is drawn; characters express emotion purely through eyes, eyebrows, head tilt, and gestures, while mustaches/beards/visors remain intact).

---

## Core Proportions & Anatomy
- **Head**: Circle centered at `(0, -218)` with radius `R = 45px` (slightly wider oval `1.04 x 0.98`), filled with `#FCF9EE` and outlined with `6.5px` `#0F4B58` (or `#3D2B24` in Kraft Storyboard World).
- **Face**: Two dot eyes (`R = 4.8px`), expressive eyebrows, and a minimal curved nose tick between the eyes. **No mouth**.
- **Torso**: Rounded Bean/Egg oval centered at `(0, -120)` with radii `rx = 49px, ry = 56px`.
- **Arms & Hands**: Quadratic Bézier curves (`7.0px` stroke) ending in **3-finger fork hands** (`draw_fork_hand`) or specialized gloves (Astronaut mittens, Boxer gloves).
- **Legs & Shoes**: Short curved bow-legs from `y = -66` to `y = 0`, ending in flat-bottom D-shaped shoes, chunky boots, flippers, or a pirate peg-leg.
